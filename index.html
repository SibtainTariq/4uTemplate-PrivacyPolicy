Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 1, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the 4u Template Android app (package com.sibtain.forutemplate), developed by Sibtain Tariq ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information.

Information processed automatically

When the app loads templates, our server receives standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, and the time of the request. We use it only to deliver the service, keep it secure, and fix problems. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Information stored on your device

  • Your favorite templates, saved locally on your phone. We do not receive this list.
  • Videos you choose to download, saved to your phone's gallery. You can delete them at any time.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to save videos to your gallery, which is used only when you tap Download. You can deny or revoke this in your phone's settings, and the rest of the app will still work.

Third-party apps and links

The "Use Template", "Share", and similar buttons open other apps or websites (for example CapCut, the Google Play Store, or a sharing app of your choice). Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Ads and analytics

The app currently shows no advertising and uses no third-party analytics or tracking tools. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change is released.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your rights

We do not keep personal profiles about users. Technical server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting. If you want to ask about, or request deletion of, any data related to you, contact us at the email below.

Content and copyright

Templates shown in the app belong to their creators. If you own content shown in the app and want it removed, email us with the template name and proof of ownership, and we will review it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
